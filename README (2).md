Dear visitor,

Welcome in. Please, take your time.

Every word you use today was once something else. *Salary* was salt money paid to Roman soldiers. *Nightmare* was a goblin that sat on sleepers' chests. *Muscle* was a little mouse, because someone once thought a flexing arm looked like one running under the skin.

**etyms** is a small museum for stories like these. Each word is kept as an artifact behind glass, and you can walk back through the rooms it has lived in, from the English of today to the oldest form anyone has recorded, and sometimes further still, to forms scholars have only reconstructed.

When you arrive, you'll find a quill at work on a sheet of papyrus, writing the letter *e* as the Phoenicians, Greeks, Norse, Armenians, Japanese and others have written it. It's there to remind you that every letter, like every word, has come a long way to reach you.

---

### How to visit

**Write a word** in the search box and press Enter, or tap the magnifying glass.

**Walk back in time.** You arrive at the entrance, in the present day. Slide the timeline to the left, or drag the word itself sideways, to move from gallery to gallery. Flick it, and it glides. Each stop tells you the language, the era and what the word meant then.

**Read the curator's note.** Under the timeline, a plaque holds the full story of the word and its relatives in other languages.

**Roll the dice** beside the search box, or tap the papyrus, and the curator will choose a word for you.

**Type "wordle"** to see today's Wordle answer, kept under a cloth until you choose to uncover it. Play first.

**Save a journey** to keep it in your browser, where it waits for you even without a connection. Or **take home a print**, an image of the word's whole journey to keep or share.

A few small courtesies: press `/` to start writing, use `←` and `→` to walk between galleries, and share any word directly with a link such as `…/#salary`.

### Where our collection comes from

Nothing here is invented. Every story is fetched, as it's needed, from collections kept by people who care about them:

- **Etymologies** come from [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org), written by thousands of volunteers and shared under [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Wordle answers** come from the open-source [wordle-answers](https://github.com/ramkrishna3245/wordle-answers) list, MIT licensed and refreshed daily.
- **Type** is set in Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans, Cormorant Garamond and Geist Mono, from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com).

No AI writes these stories, and no account is needed. The journeys you save stay in your own browser and never leave it.
